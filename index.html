H Harness

H Harness is an internal market research tool. It helps us decide which mobile apps to build next by combining public App Store data with keyword demand, and then drafts product specs and designs for the apps we choose.

How it uses Google Ads

With the permission of the account owner, H Harness connects to our own Google Ads account through the Google Ads API and reads Keyword Planner metrics: average monthly searches, competition and top-of-page bid ranges for keywords we are researching. It does not create, change or pause campaigns, does not manage anyone else's accounts and is not offered to third parties.

Who uses it: only the developer's own team, for our own apps.
What it reads: keyword research metrics from our own Google Ads account.
What it stores: the keyword metrics it retrieves, on our own machines.
